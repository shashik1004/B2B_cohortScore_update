1Executive summary

The B2B score asks one question about each correspondent and co-issue client: do its loans pay off faster, and get recaptured less, than look-alike loans from other lenders? The new method sorts every loan into a fixed peer cohort when the loan is priced. It then compares the client's actual prepayment speed with the actual speed of all peer loans in the same cohorts. The legacy method compared it with a model forecast.

WHAT CHANGED VS THE LEGACY SCORE

  • The benchmark is realized, not modeled. Legacy CPRScore divided client CPR by the valuation model's projected CPR. That forecast missed by different amounts for each loan type (VA: model 8.6% vs peers' actual 22.4%), which reordered clients. The legacy portfolio score was 0.64, not 1.00.
  • A loan's peer group is set once and never changes. The cohort comes from the loan's attributes at lock (Correspondent) or funding (Co-Issue and Consumer Direct). It stays fixed for the life of the loan.
  • The fallbacks are relative to the portfolio and everything is auditable. Clients with no prepayment are scaled off the portfolio score. Every published CPRScore can be rebuilt in Excel from the audit table Analytics_Rpt.md.B2B_CPRScore_Audit.

2What the B2B score is & how it is used

Purpose

Onity buys and services loans from correspondent and co-issue clients. The value of that servicing depends mostly on how long the loan stays on the books (prepayment) and on whether Onity keeps the borrower when the loan pays off (recapture). Two clients can deliver loans with the same rate and FICO that still behave very differently after funding. The B2B score measures that client-specific behavior so pricing and purchase decisions can reflect it.

The three outputs

OutputQuestion it answersHow to read it
CPRScoreDo this client's loans prepay faster or slower than peer loans in the same cohorts?Lower is better. 1.00 = same as peers; 0.72 = 28% slower; 1.33 = 33% faster.
RecapScoreWhen this client's loans pay off, does Onity recapture more or less balance than the benchmark recapture rate?Higher is better. 1.00 = at benchmark.
TotalScoreTaking both together, is this client's servicing worth more or less than average?Higher is better. RecapScore ÷ CPRScore.
F / N / U tagWhere does the client stand relative to the whole book?F Favorable ≥ 1.25 × portfolio · N Neutral · U Unfavorable < 0.75 × portfolio

How it is used

  • Client pricing and purchase mix. The F / N / U tag feeds B2B pricing strategy. The Oct-2026 review set an illustrative target buy mix of 85% Favorable / 10% Neutral / 5% Unfavorable. At the current ~3% overall win rate, that needs a 5.9% win rate on Favorable volume.
  • Account management. Reports carry each client's Account Manager (East / West / Central) and seller IDs by sub-channel, so AEs can act on a score.
  • Drill-downs. The same scores are cut by state, FICO, LTV, DTI, note rate, product, average loan balance, channel and client, plus Client × State and Client × State × Product views. These cuts show where a client is fast or slow.
  • Executive reporting. Monthly production metrics track prediction accuracy, tag stability and purchase mix (see Section 11).
In one sentence
A client scores well only when its loans prepay slower than loans like its own, or when it recaptures more. The comparison is always against what peer loans actually did in the same months, never against a forecast.

3Score architecture

1
Assign each loan to a cohort
Once, at lock or funding, from five loan features. Stored in B2B_revamp_cohorts.
2
Measure the client's actual CPR
Annualized balance run-off of the client's cohort-assigned loans over the score window.
3
Build the peer benchmark
Actual CPR of all loans, from all clients, in the cohorts where the client has loans.
4
CPRScore = actual ÷ benchmark
Below 1 means slower than peers, which is good.
5
TotalScore = RecapScore ÷ CPRScore
Then tag F / N / U against the portfolio TotalScore.
TotalScore = RecapScore ÷ CPRScore   |   CPRScore = Client ActualCPR ÷ Peer-cohort ActualCPR   |   RecapScore = ActualRecap ÷ CohortRecapExact formulas, windows and fallback rules are in Section 7.
The valuation model sets who the peers are, not how fast they should prepay
Cohorts were designed with the valuation model's projected ModelCPR, which identified the features that drive prepayment and showed where to draw band edges. Scoring never uses a projected CPR. The benchmark is always the observed run-off of peer loans in the score window. The cohort statistics Mean_CPR / Std_CPR are kept in the tables for reference only.

5.3 Band design and merge algorithm

Starting bands (full Cartesian space = 1,728 cells)

DimensionStarting bands#Merged?
LoanTypeCONV (incl. CONV-PMI), FHA, VA, OTHERS (USDA / FARM / unknown)4Never
RemainingTermBand≤ 240 mo, > 240 mo, Missing (null or negative)3Never

4 × 3 × 2 × 4 × 18 = 1,728 cells. Every combination keeps a slot, including empty ones, so any future loan always has a cohort. All intervals are right-inclusive (240 falls in ≤ 240).

Merge rules (business parameters)

Within each fixed LoanType × Term × FICO segment, the algorithm first merges adjacent balance bands. It then merges adjacent spread bands inside each resulting balance group. It repeatedly merges the single best adjacent pair until no trigger fires.

ParameterValueRole
MIN_COHORT_N_FLOOR1,500Forced merge. A band with fewer than 1,500 observations must merge with a neighbor. Forced merges skip the inflation guard, so empty and tiny bands can always be absorbed (full coverage).
SE_MEAN_TARGET0.25 CPR ptPrecision trigger. Merge if the standard error of the mean (Std ÷ √N) is above 0.25.
MEAN_MERGE_TOL0.5 CPR ptSimilarity trigger. Merge neighbors whose mean CPRs are within 0.5 pt of each other.
STD_INFLATION_MAX1.20×Guard. Reject a non-forced merge if the pooled std would be more than 1.2× the tighter neighbor's std.
CV_FLAG_THRESHOLD0.25Reporting flag only. Marks cohorts whose std is ≥ 25% of their mean.

Merge priority: forced, then imprecise, then similar. Ties go to the lowest std inflation, then the smallest mean gap. Empty cells borrow interpolated means from their nearest populated neighbors, so they merge toward the closer-CPR side. Pooled statistics combine within- and between-band variance.

Actual CPR therefore measures voluntary prepayment: payoffs and large curtailments.

7.2 CPRScore: peer cohort method

Within the score window, for any report group g (a client, a state, a Client × State cell, etc.), using only cohort-assigned loan-months:

ActualCPRg = 1 − ( Σg EndPrinBal ÷ Σg BeginPrinBal )12The client's own pooled run-off across all of its cohort-assigned loan-months, annualized.
PeerCPRg = 1 − ( Σc ∈ C(g) CohortEndBalc ÷ Σc ∈ C(g) CohortBeginBalc )12C(g) = the set of cohorts in which group g has loans. Cohort balances are pooled over all clients in the same performance frame, and each cohort is counted once (code label: Cohort_ActualCPR).
CPRScoreg = ActualCPRg ÷ PeerCPRgExported as Client_ActualCPR, Cohort_ActualCPR, CPRScore. For the Total row, C(g) is every cohort, so the portfolio CPRScore is 1.00 by construction.
How the benchmark is weighted
Each cohort in C(g) enters the benchmark with its total peer balance, not with the client's share of its own balance. A client with even one loan-month in a large cohort brings that whole cohort's balance into its benchmark. This matches the published calculation in the Oct-2026 deck (e.g. "every lender's loans, same 81 cohorts: 1 − ($812B ÷ $822B)12") and is checked in the notebook (Validation 2: max difference 0.00e+00 across 975 clients). See Section 12.

7.3 RecapScore and TotalScore

Recapture uses payoff loan-months with PIFBal > 0 and PIFReason ∈ {Purchase, Rate/Term Refinance, Cash-Out Refinance}:

ActualRecapg = Σ RecapBal ÷ Σ PIFBal   ·   CohortRecapg = Σ (CohortRecap × PIFBal) ÷ Σ PIFBal   ·   RecapScoreg = ActualRecapg ÷ CohortRecapgRecapBal = new-loan amount funded by Onity on the payoff. CohortRecap = loan-level benchmark recapture rate, read from B2BPricingHist2.
TotalScoreg = RecapScoreg ÷ CPRScoreg

The recapture formulas are the same as in the legacy score. What changed is the source of CohortRecap. The legacy score read B2BPricingHist, where the benchmark recapture rate is computed by payoff month × product category × payoff purpose × peer-recapture eligibility (Finance_DM.md.usp_md_B2BPricing). The new score reads B2BPricingHist2. Per the Oct-2026 deck, its benchmark is set "at the overall portfolio level, by payoff reason and payoff month" (Recapture V2).

7.4 Fallbacks and edge cases

MIN_COHORT_LOANS = 20 is the single loan-count cutoff (N) used by the CPR default and by the zero-CPR fallback.

#SituationRule applied
1Group has no cohort-assigned loans (PLS / unmapped only)CPRScore = 1.0 (neutral). Client_ActualCPR is shown on all loans. Unmapped loans never enter either side of the CPR ratio.
2Group has fewer than 20 loan-months in the score windowCPRScore = 1.0.
3Peer benchmark ≤ 0 or undefinedCPRScore = 1.0.
4Recapture: benchmark null / 0, fewer than 5 payoff loan-months, or ActualRecap undefinedRecapScore = 1.0.
5RecapScore = 0 (eligible payoffs but nothing recaptured)TotalScore = 0.5 ÷ CPRScore.
6Zero prepayment: Client_ActualCPR = 0 (any non-Total row)TotalScore = Portfolio TotalScore × m(n), where n = CPR loan-months and m(n) = 1.0 if n < 20, else 1.25 × log10(n). Examples: n = 20 → 1.63×, n = 100 → 2.50×, n = 1,000 → 3.75×. In Client × State × Product views the benchmark is the TotalScore of that product.
7F / N / U tagAssigned only to MetricName = 'Client' rows of the All-product report (rawTotal).
Why rule 6 changed
With no payoffs at all, CPRScore = 0 and RecapScore ÷ CPRScore is undefined. The legacy score replaced it with an absolute 1.25 × log10(n). That was not comparable once the portfolio score was no longer about 1. The new rule anchors the reward to the current portfolio TotalScore and scales it with the client's evidence, so a perfect record earns more credit the more loan-months back it up.

8Legacy score vs peer cohort score

Legacy: model benchmark

Client CPR ÷ balance-weighted average of loan-level ModelCPR (the valuation model's projected lifetime CPR from Finance_DM.md.MSRValuesLoanLevel, stored in B2BPricingHist).

Measures how a client did against a forecast. Any forecast bias for a loan type or rate environment passes straight into every client's score.

Peer cohort: realized benchmark

Client CPR ÷ pooled actual CPR of all peer loans in the same fixed cohorts, over the same months.

Measures how a client did against loans like its own in the same market. Market-wide moves cancel, and the portfolio scores 1.00.

10Operating procedure

Monthly run

  1. Confirm upstream data. Month-end B2BPricingHist and B2BPricingHist2 are loaded for the new AsofDt.
  2. Stage 2, APPEND. In B2B_revamp_cohorts_build_FundDt.ipynb set RUN_MODE = 'APPEND' and NEW_MONTHS = ['YYYY-MM-DD'] (or [] to find missing months automatically). Run all cells. Check: row count matches the source, mapped share is about 100%, and the invariance check shows 0 loans with multiple cohorts.
  3. Stage 3, score. In B2B_Reporting_cohort_score_customDates.ipynb set pi_BeginScoreDt / pi_EndScoreDt (performance months) and leave funding dates as None for the trailing-12-month default. Run all cells.
  4. Check the output. Total-row CPRScore = 1.0; report-month cohort coverage about 100%; audit reconciliations at 0 difference; review top clients and tag changes.
  5. Publish. Distribute the Excel file. Push B2B_CPRScore_Audit and, once the Excel file is verified, B2B_ClientScore_final. Both pushes replace the existing tables.

Cohort refresh (model review)

  1. Point Stage 1 at new training tapes (TRAIN_SRCS). Re-run the driver, sensitivity and held-out checks, and document any parameter changes.
  2. Export the artifacts under a new schema or version. Do not overwrite the current ones until the new set is approved.
  3. Copy the approved artifacts to cohorts_band/ and run Stage 2 with RUN_MODE = 'COHORT_REFRESH', so all history is re-scored on the same basis.
  4. Run Stage 3, compare against the prior run, and update this document (change log, Sections 5–7).

12Assumptions, limitations & open items

Design assumptions

  • The five features capture the main expected differences in prepayment between loans. What is left between a client and its peers is attributed to the client (origination quality, borrower targeting, retention practices).
  • The cohort is fixed at the pricing decision. Later changes in balance, rate environment or remaining term do not move a loan. Those changes affect all peers in the cohort equally.
  • Cohorts were learned on seasoned active loans (Jun / Jul-2026 tapes) but are applied to attributes at lock or funding. Band edges are therefore used as a peer-grouping device, not as calibrated CPR forecasts.

Known limitations

  • Benchmark weighting. The peer benchmark weights each cohort the client touches by its full peer balance, not by the client's own mix (7.2). A client with a small position in a large, fast or slow cohort pulls that cohort's full weight into its benchmark. A client-mix-weighted variant would be a candidate enhancement for the next review.
  • Training window. Two monthly snapshots in a single rate environment. Held-out tests are by loan, not out-of-time. SE_Mean treats repeat monthly observations as independent, so it understates uncertainty.
  • Within-cohort dispersion. Loan-weighted CV is 0.265. 77 of 109 cohorts (51.2% of UPB) carry at least one caution flag (SE, count floor or CV > 0.25), mostly CV. Forced merges of sparse bands can combine different speeds (most often FHA / VA ≤ 240 months and OTHERS).
  • Sparse segments. 7 populated cohorts stay below the 1,500-observation floor, because merges never cross LoanType, term or FICO. 7 Missing-term rules have no training estimate. Current production loans do not land in them.
  • Small clients. Fewer than 20 loan-months gives a neutral CPRScore (1.0). The zero-prepay multiplier jumps from 1.0× to 1.63× at n = 20.

13Frequently asked questions

What is a "peer cohort"?

A group of loans that share the same loan type, remaining-term band, FICO band, balance group and rate-vs-market group when they were priced. There are 116 such groups. A client's loans are compared only with other lenders' loans in the same groups.

Does the score use a prepayment model?

Only to design the cohorts, which means choosing features and band edges. The benchmark itself is the actual run-off of peer loans in the same months. No forecast enters the score.

Why is the portfolio CPRScore exactly 1.00?

For the Total row, the peers are every loan in the book, so the client and benchmark CPR are the same number. A client's score therefore shows its position relative to the book, and a market-wide refinance wave does not lower everyone's score.

Can a loan change cohort over time?

No. The cohort is set from attributes at lock (Correspondent) or funding (Co-Issue). Every month is checked for invariance. Cohorts change only when the whole set of definitions is refreshed, and then all history is re-scored.

Why use the lock date for Correspondent?

That is when the rate (and so the refinance incentive against the market) is set and the purchase is priced. Using the lock-date market rate measures the loan's incentive as the pricing decision saw it.

Is lower or higher better?

CPRScore: lower is better (slower prepayment). RecapScore and TotalScore: higher is better. Tags: Favorable is TotalScore ≥ 1.25 × portfolio, Unfavorable is < 0.75 × portfolio.

What happens to small clients or clients with no payoffs?

Under 20 loan-months gives CPRScore = 1.0 (neutral). If a client had no prepayment at all, TotalScore = portfolio TotalScore × (1.0 if n < 20, else 1.25 × log10 n). See 7.4.

Why did my client's tag change from the legacy score?

The client's own CPR did not change. The yardstick did. The legacy model under-predicted prepayment by different amounts per loan type (most for VA). Clients heavy in under-predicted segments improve. Clients that prepay faster than their own look-alikes fall.

Are PLS or unmapped loans scored?

They count in volume metrics but are left out of both sides of the CPR ratio. Report-month coverage is about 100%.

How do I reproduce a client's CPRScore?

Use cprAuditWide or md.B2B_CPRScore_Audit. They hold the client's balances and loan-months per cohort and the pooled peer balances. Client CPR = 1 − (Σ client end ÷ Σ client begin)12. Peer CPR = 1 − (Σ cohort end ÷ Σ cohort begin)12 over the client's cohorts. Divide one by the other.

Are the FICO / LTV / rate bands in the report the cohort bands?

No. Report bands (FICO 780+ / 740 / 700 …, LTV, DTI, note-rate and average-loan-balance buckets) are display slices. Cohort bands are listed in Appendix A.
